Grand Theft Auto: Harboursville

An original browser game. This does not run the Grand-V engine and is not affiliated with Rockstar or Take-Two. Grand-V's GitHub repo is a launcher only: it has no guns, avatars, or physics to reuse.

Drive the gold rings. F steps out into an original avatar. J or Fire shoots arcing pellets at the orange buoys. Space is the handbrake in the car and a jump on foot.

Road, water, park, pier, and building footprints are simplified OpenStreetMap data © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL.

The Opera House shells, Harbour Bridge arch, car, water shader, and lighting are original stylized models. Heights without an OSM tag are estimates.

No aerial photos and no photogrammetry. Three.js is MIT licensed. Grand-V was not reused. Both public branches are an unlicensed launcher with empty releases. The avatar, pellet sidearm, and gravity are original stand-ins, not RAGE or GTA assets.
